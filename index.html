About "total profile engagement"

LinkedIn does not give per-profile engagement totals (all posts, likes, comments) to logged-out visitors, and the Ads API does not cover members' organic posts. So this tab shows what we can see publicly. (1) Reactions and comments on the promoted post, from the public post page. (2) How many of each person's last ~15–20 public activity items are their own posts (the rest are likes or reposts), which shows how actively they publish. (3) What they usually post about, read from that activity. Getting full profile engagement would need either LinkedIn creator analytics shared by each TL, or a logged-in scrape. We did neither.

✅ What predicts success

  1. Image post, not video. All of the Top 6 posts are banners. Banner $627 vs video $1,198 per Hard SU.
  2. Show proof: a number or a named agent. "200+ hours of work" (Rachel Woods, $378), "Brittany… 9 meetings in 4 days" (Rachel Leigh), "half my team is agents" (Beatrice). Of the Top 6, 5 have one, vs 0 of the bottom 6.
  3. A face in the visual. The creator's own photo, or the agent drawn as a character. 5 of the Top 6 have one. Stock UI link cards and text title cards come last.
  4. Keep it to ~150–250 words. The Top 6 median is 253 words (1,431 characters), vs 234 for the bottom 6. Across all 27 qualifying posts: up to 200 words = $592/Hard SU, 201–300 = $936, over 300 = $738. Under 200 is clearly best. Above 200 there's no further gradient, and even without Rachel Woods, ≤200 words is still $791. 4 of the Top 6 are ≤210 words. The 2 longer winners (Kate Kenner 349, Asaf 301) are carried by short lines and a strong hook. Write it in short lines (median 22 line breaks), with a one-line hook and a "humans stay in charge" line. Short alone isn't enough: Antoni (126 words) and Moni (196) were short but used a stock card or a launch video.
  5. Organic traction first. The Top 6 had a median of ~380 public reactions before and during promotion, vs ~57. A post that the creator's own network ignores rarely converts on paid.
  6. GB delivery is 42% cheaper per Hard SU ($492 vs $849), mostly from CPM.
  7. Gender: women lead, but it is not clean. Female $646 vs male $773. Without the two Rachels, female = $974 overall, but on banners women still beat men ($868 vs $737). Women hold 4 of the top 6 and 4 of the bottom 6 posts.

⛔ What not to do

  1. Don't use a stock product-UI link card as the visual. Antoni ($2,516) and Jonathan ($1,388) both showed the same generic monday board screenshot. No face, no story, and the lowest engagement of all (0.14% / 0.26%).
  2. Don't promote talking-head video, even with a real face. Michelle ($2,028) and Nicole ($1,417) are selfie or mic videos. Nicole got a healthy 2.45% engagement rate and still cost ~3× the winners. The face works in a still photo, not in video.
  3. Don't open with a confession or an opinion that never pays off. "Honest confession: AI keeps getting pushed down the list" (Michelle), "Confession time: I was not an AI early adopter" (Jillian), "There's a lot of noise around AI in SaaS" (Jonathan). None of the bottom 6 has a number or a named agent. The winners open with a claim and prove it ("200+ hours", "9 meetings in 4 days", "half my team is agents").
  4. Don't promote long posts. The bottom 6 median is ~276 words, vs ~195 for the top 6. Nicole (504 words) and Jillian (350) are the longest losers. Aim for about 150–250 words in short lines.
  5. Don't promote a post its own network ignored. The bottom 6 had a median of ~84 public reactions, vs ~590 for the top 6. If a post has under ~150 reactions after 3–4 days, pick another one.
  6. Don't promote stale posts. Jonathan and Antoni went live ~3 weeks after posting. 8+ day lag = $1,171/Hard SU in the US, vs $526 for 4–7 days.
  7. No corporate launch video. The white "Your team" launch video (Moni, Hazel, Jillian, all $1,290–$2,694) reads as an ad, not a person.
  8. No US video. US Video $1,405/Hard SU at a $29 CPM.
  9. Don't keep dead posts live. Moni Houser, Lital Nof-Sabbag, Antoni Gmurczyk, Jonathan Guerra: under 0.4% engagement, $29,485 spend, $1,638/Hard SU. Cut after ~$2K if engagement stays under 0.5%.
  10. Don't pay for follower count. 50K+ creators cost $931/Hard SU vs $779 for creators under 10K.
  11. Don't compare TLA with blended regular. LAN makes regular look 2× better than it really is.
