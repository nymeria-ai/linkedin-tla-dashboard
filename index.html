About "total profile engagement"

LinkedIn does not give per-profile engagement totals (all posts, likes, comments) to logged-out visitors, and the Ads API does not cover members' organic posts. So this tab shows what we can see publicly. (1) Reactions and comments on the promoted post, from the public post page. (2) How many of each person's last ~15–20 public activity items are their own posts (the rest are likes or reposts), which shows how actively they publish. (3) What they usually post about, read from that activity. Getting full profile engagement would need either LinkedIn creator analytics shared by each TL, or a logged-in scrape. We did neither.

⚔️ TLA vs regular: the headline

  • Apples-to-apples (same US skills/seniority audience, banner): TLA $693 vs regular $1,543 per Hard SU. TLA is 2.2× cheaper. Over the same months: $680 vs $1,440.
  • The old line "TLA is ~1.8× the cost of regular" ($680 vs $368) is only true when LAN ad sets are included. Those serve off-LinkedIn at ~$4 CPM, so they aren't a fair benchmark. Without LAN, regular = $1,553.
  • TLA video in the US ($1,495) is no better than the regular banner. The TLA advantage comes from member banner posts.

✅ What predicts success

  1. Image post, not video. All of the Top 6 posts are banners. Banner $590 vs video $1,238 per Hard SU.
  2. Show proof: a number or a named agent. "200+ hours of work" (Rachel Woods, $392), "Brittany… 9 meetings in 4 days" (Rachel Leigh), "half my team is agents" (Beatrice). Of the Top 6, 3 have one, vs 0 of the bottom 6.
  3. A face in the visual. The creator's own photo, or the agent drawn as a character. 5 of the Top 6 have one. Stock UI link cards and text title cards come last.
  4. Short lines, ~195 words, a one-line hook, and a "humans stay in charge" line.
  5. Organic traction first. The Top 6 had a median of ~590 public reactions before and during promotion, vs ~84. A post that the creator's own network ignores rarely converts on paid.
  6. GB delivery is 32% cheaper per Hard SU ($547 vs $808), mostly from CPM.
  7. Gender is not a driver. Female $623 vs male $823, but without the two Rachels, female = $886.

⛔ What not to do

  1. No hiring posts. Jonathan ($1,388) and Antoni ($2,516) promoted job posts with a stock UI link card, ~3 weeks after posting.
