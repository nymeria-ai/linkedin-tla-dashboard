✅ What predicts success

  1. Banner, not video. Banners $590 vs video $1,238 per Hard SU (2.1×). 8 of 8 video-only TLs are above the TLA average.
  2. A concrete, countable result. "AI agents did 200+ hours of work" (Rachel Woods, $392) and "half my team is monday agents" (Beatrice, $625). The Productivity-proof theme costs $421/Hard SU vs $680 overall.
  3. Posts people actually engage with. TLs with ≥2% engagement rate: $511/Hard SU. TLs under 0.4%: $1,638. Engagement isn't the goal, but a dead post never converts.
  4. GB delivery. GB $547 vs US $808, mostly thanks to a lower CPM ($14.70 vs $23.41). Every category is cheaper in GB.
  5. Promote 4–6 days after the post. $568/Hard SU vs $871 same-day, $751 at 1–3 days and $1,764 at 7+ days. The post has organic proof (likes/comments) by then, but it still feels fresh.
  6. Female TLs $623 vs male $823, but this is all Rachel Woods + Rachel Leigh. Without them, female TLs are $886/Hard SU, about the same as male. Gender is not a real driver.

⛔ What not to do

  1. Don't run video in the US. US Video $1,495/Hard SU at a $30.08 CPM, the worst of the 4 ad sets.
  2. Don't promote corporate announcements reworded as a person's post. "AI work platform for people & agents" (Jonathan, Antoni): Platform-evangelism theme $824. Antoni is at 0.14% engagement and $2,516.
  3. Don't keep low-engagement posts live. Jonathan Guerra, Antoni Gmurczyk, Lital Nof-Sabbag, Moni Houser: $29,485 spend, under 0.4% engagement, $1,638/Hard SU. Cut them after ~$2K if engagement stays under 0.5%.
  4. Don't buy follower count. 50K+ creators (Beatrice, Tom, Matt, Nicole): $785/Hard SU, no better than TLs with under 10K followers ($795).
  5. Don't promote old posts. 7+ day lag: $1,764/Hard SU.
  6. Embossers in the US don't work: $2,834/Hard SU (3 SU). In GB the same posts get $420.

🔁 Trends / common ground of the winning posts

😩 Personal pain→🤖 A named agent→📈 A concrete result→🧑‍✈️ "Humans still in charge"→❓ A question to the reader
  • The top posts read like a personal story with a number in it, not like a launch. The winners (Rachel Woods, Kate Kenner, Beatrice, Rachel Leigh, Lior) all name what the agent did, in plain words.
  • Reassurance beats hype. The best posts say outright that people stay in control ("agents handle the rest, you set direction").
  • Banner creative + a strong first line: CPM is ~30% lower than video and the hook does the work.
  • Same post, different market: the same post usually costs 30–60% less per Hard SU in GB than in the US. Judge a post on both markets before killing it.
