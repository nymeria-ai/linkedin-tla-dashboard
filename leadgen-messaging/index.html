What worked in past lead form campaigns (account 507706187, in-platform, all time to Oct 18 2026)

Top 70 lead-gen creatives by lead count (oneClickLeads), CPL = spend / leads:

  • Content offers (eBook/guide/playbook/video series): 48 ads, 10,465 leads, CPL $117, form CVR 14%
  • Product/value pitch (no offer): 16 ads, 1,923 leads, CPL $826, form CVR 2.3%
  • Demo / consultation request: 6 ads, 376 leads, CPL $2,184, form CVR 1.2%

Best single ads:

  • PMO eBook 'The manager's guide to executing successful projects in 2024' (US): 1,217 leads, CPL $114, form CVR 20%. Hook: 'Only 35% of projects are completed successfully (Harvard Business Review).'
  • 'Increase your ROI' project eBook (US/EMEA, 2022-23): ~3,800 leads across versions, CPL $65-$133. Hook: 'Why do so many strategic projects fail to produce positive results?'
  • Marketing eBooks 'Build a content team…' and '8 marketing metrics to track': CPL $38-$78, form CVR 22-36%
  • InMail eBook offers: CPL $37-$96, form CVR 33-45%
  • Operations product pitch 'From chasing tasks to driving business growth': CPL $1,048-$2,258. No offer = expensive.

Winning pattern: question or stat hook in the first line, 'Free eBook:' + clear title in the headline, manager/PM framing, one short benefit line, ✅ bullets only for a 'what's inside' list.
Caveat: these are lead counts only. Lead quality (SQL/CPS) is not in-platform, so the demo control (V6) is there to compare quality, not volume.

Test setup

  • One angle per ad. Each variant has 3 track versions (PMO / Marketing / Ops), same structure, only the hook/headline/asset changes.
  • V1-V3 use the same CTA (Download) so the test isolates the angle.
  • Character counts checked: all hooks ≤150, full intro ≤600, headline ≤70, form headline ≤60, form description ≤160.
  • Anything in [PLACEHOLDER] must be filled with a verified stat/name before launch.
